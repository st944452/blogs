Steel Mountain

Difficulty: Medium • Platform: Windows

This sample post gives you a ready-made structure for publishing room walkthroughs. Swap in your own commands, screenshots, and notes after each session.

Enumeration

Start with a concise summary of the target and the first scans you ran. Keep this section practical so readers can quickly understand the attack surface you identified. 

nmap -sC -sV -oN initial-scan.txt TARGET_IP

Initial Foothold

Describe the vulnerable service or application, why it looked promising, and how you confirmed the exploit path. Mention failed attempts too if they taught you something useful.

Privilege Escalation

Explain the path from user access to administrator or SYSTEM with enough detail for later review. This is often the section that becomes most valuable when you revisit old boxes. 

whoami /priv
systeminfo
winPEAS.exe

Lessons Learned

End with a short reflection. Focus on the patterns you want to remember: a service fingerprint, a Windows escalation vector, or a workflow improvement for the next room.
